# Unicode

These are the Unicode characters representing the different Chess pieces:

- White King: ♔
- White Queen: ♕
- White Rook: ♖
- White Bishop: ♗
- White Knight: ♘
- White Pawn: ♙

- Black King: ♚
- Black Queen: ♛
- Black Rook: ♜
- Black Bishop: ♝
- Black Knight: ♞
- Black Pawn: ♟

Note: you need a font that includes these glyphs in order to see anything.
